The status LED

The RGB LED on the DevKit says what the board is doing. Watch it for a few seconds - the rhythm matters as much as the colour.

The board measures and stores whatever the LED shows. Without Wi-Fi or server, every five-minute record goes to its own flash - about 57 days of them - and is sent, oldest first, once the connection is back. A current reading always goes first.

The two buttons on the DevKit

Can the buttons reset the board? Only partly, on purpose.
  • RST restarts. Settings, the heel/pitch zero and all stored measurements survive; only the five-minute window being filled at that moment is lost. The sensors keep their power - a sensor that has hung itself needs the supply taken away (USB or 12 V), not RST.
  • BOOT 8 s resets the access point password and nothing else. Marina Wi-Fi, server settings and data stay.
  • There is no factory reset on a button, so nothing can be wiped by leaning on the box in a locker. A complete wipe - every setting and every unsent measurement - needs a laptop on USB: pio run -d board -e boathub -t erase, then flash the firmware again.
  • BOOT held while pressing RST starts the chip's firmware download mode: LED dark, no Wi-Fi. Press RST alone to leave it.

The configuration page

  1. Join the Wi-Fi BOOT-NETZ with phone or tablet. The page usually opens by itself; otherwise go to http://192.168.4.1. The phone may say "no internet" - that is normal, the board does not route to the internet.
  2. Change what is needed and press Save and restart. The board restarts within a few seconds and BOOT-NETZ drops briefly. An empty password field keeps the stored password.

The page answers only on BOOT-NETZ, never from the marina network. Its first line shows the Wi-Fi state, the board's address in the marina network, whether the server is connected and which alarms are raised. http://192.168.4.1/status gives the same as text.

Level now - its own button at the bottom of the page. Records the boat's present attitude as zero for heel and pitch. Only with the boat at rest and loaded as usual, and again whenever the box has been remounted. Refused for the first half minute after a start, while the motion sensor settles. Heel and pitch are not reported at all until this has been done once.

When something looks wrong

  • BOOT-NETZ password unknown - hold BOOT 8 s; it is the default again.
  • BOOT-NETZ drops for a moment - the marina access point changed channel, and the board follows it. Rejoin; nothing is lost.
  • One sensor missing on the dashboard - its cable or terminal. It comes back by itself once it answers again, without a restart. If it does not, take the power away for a few seconds.
  • No heel and pitch - not levelled yet: press Level now.
  • Battery bay at 100 % humidity for hours - the sensor is wet. Check that its opening faces sideways or down, out of drips.
  • Records arrive late, in a batch - normal after an outage; they carry their own time.
