Holding the board

Component side up, turned so that WLAN MODULE UP reads normally. PWR 12V is then bottom left, the Seatalk terminal bottom centre and the three DS18B20 terminals along the right edge. Left, right, top and bottom mean this position throughout.

  • The square pad is pin 1. On a diode that is the cathode with the band, on an electrolytic the positive lead.
  • Orange means the direction matters. Band, minus stripe, dot or flat face go where the drawing is orange.
  • Arrows at the terminals show where the wire goes in: always from the board edge.
  • Hollow pads stay empty. C7 to C12 and JP2 are left unfitted on purpose.
  • Hatched is room for a module standing over the board: the two ADS1115 and the DevKit's USB sockets.
  • Low before tall. Resistors, then capacitors, diodes, semiconductors, electrolytics and terminals, so the board lies flat while you solder.
  • The converter U2 goes in last. Until then the board runs from the DevKit's USB, so everything else is tested before the converter is at risk.
